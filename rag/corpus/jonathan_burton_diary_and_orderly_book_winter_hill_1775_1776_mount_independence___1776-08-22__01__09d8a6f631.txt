Thursday August 22" after we had Baked our Bread and Dressed our
Provition we took in about 30 Boards att the Mills and Seet off from the
creek and got into the Lake in about two hours and % then the wind not
favouring us we had hard work to get into any harbour where we could
Lay Secure but by the winds Shifting we Secured our Selves in a Little
Creek that Night and Lodged in our boats
