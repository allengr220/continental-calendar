29

Provided the Stuf but orders came that Eighteen Men from our company
was to be draughted to go as Mariners on board the Rogallies to Cruse
on the Lake Down towards St Johns. Put by our building this Day

Sunday August y® 11 1776 this Day Prov’ to be Rainey all Day.
Now we begin to See Sick men Laying on the cold ground without anything to cover them but their Blankets (This Place is called Mount Independent it is a high Hill of Land Laying S: East from Ticonderoga
about a mild and 4 from the fort)
