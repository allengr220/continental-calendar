Fryday August 23 we Set off from our Lodging very Earley and got
up to Crown pint about ten oclock in the forenoon and after takeing
Sum Refreshment there we Set of and Landed at Putnams Point (So
called) and then we took our Dinners from there we Set of with a fair
wind and Landed at Ticonderoga about two hours by Sun and after I
had Delivered the boats the Party was Dismised and came to the camp
that night where I had the Malencoly News of the Death of Caleb Putnam who Died the 22" and was Bured a few hours Before I came into
the camp—
