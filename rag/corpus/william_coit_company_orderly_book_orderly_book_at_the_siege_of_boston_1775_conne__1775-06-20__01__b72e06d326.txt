June 20]

Regmential orders

that every Compy Make out and Return to the Adju- 
tant a Compleat Return of the Number Men and Mifs- 
ing wounded of Guns and Blanckets Lost in the Last 
Ingagment at Charlestown this Day or by 9 oclock to 
morrow Morning
