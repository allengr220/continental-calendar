Saturday Sept’ 21 this Day I was on the mane guard Capt Humphries
of Col? Wingates Regt Capt of the guard S# guard was Kep'! at the Citidal—

Sunday Sept* 22"4 this Day Nothing Meterial hapned—
Monday Sept' 23" this Day we Began to Pay off the men their Billiting money and Mileage A man of Patersons Regt Had his Right Lege

almost Shot off by accidente This Day we do duty in Gin' Reeds Brigade
