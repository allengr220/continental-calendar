Monday August y® 5 we Set of from Rutland fort the Place formerly
cald oter creek on our March for Scheensborough and after a Long and
worrysome March of about twenty five Milds About half of the comp’
got into the first house in S* Town which was about 3 Milds from South
Bay or the Mill
