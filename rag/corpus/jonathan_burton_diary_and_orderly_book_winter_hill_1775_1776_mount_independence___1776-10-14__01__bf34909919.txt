Monday October 14 about two hours before Day the whole of our
Brigade was mustred again and went Down to the Lines and after going
through the fireings at the Alarm Post we Returnd to the camp and was
Dismissed and Every man in our Regt was ordered on forteauge- in
throughing up a brestworke Round the camps and in Scouting Parties—
