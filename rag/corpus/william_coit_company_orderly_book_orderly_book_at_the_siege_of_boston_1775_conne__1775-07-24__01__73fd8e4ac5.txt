Head Quarters Cambrigde July 24th 1775

Parole Salsbury Counterfign Cumberland 
It being thought proper to Distingufh the Majrs 
from the Brigadear Gen11 by som perticuler mark for 
the Future the Majrs Gen11 will weare a Broad purpel 
Ribband

Notwithftanding the Genlls orders Marking the Dis- 
tinguishing officers Adcamps &amp;C the Genolls are Freo 
quently stoped by the Centrnels which Can only happen 
by the Cap4 Neglecting to Read the orders to there 
Refpedive Companys If any Gen11 officers adecamps 
or Majrs Brigade is again stoped through the Ignornce 
of the Centernels the Capts will be Confidered Refponfble
