General orders April 23d 1775

That Each Adjutant Serf Majr and Each Ser* be Imme- 
diatly provided With orderly Books in orders Regularly 
to Enter the orders of the army.

that the Adjutant General obtain as Soon as posable 
a Compleate Return of the army in orders to Form an
