Cambrigde Head Quarters 28 July 1775

Parole Cumberland Counterfign Brookline 
the Surgans of Learnords heaths Phineys Litles &amp; 
Perfons Regmts having Negle&amp;ed to Deliver in there

with and the Surgans of those Regmts are to be more 
Exa6t in there obeadance to orders
