Tuesday August 20" after we had cooked our Brekfast we Set off for
Crownpint and after Landing there and Gitting a Pass from the Gen! we
Devided our Provition and -ent Down the Lake from the Pinte about
five Mild and then we Landed and Drest our Suppers and Lodged there
that Night
