After order of January 2™4 14476

The men who have ben Draughted for the secrit Expedition are to
Return to their Duty till further orders yet they are by no means to considder themselves as freed fromthe Obligation they have Laid themselves
under but upon Proper notice Given are to prepare to Execute the com-

mands that may then be given.
