if ryday August 9 1776 about Ten oclock this morning we Set off
from Skeensborough in boats for the tie and about Sunset we arrived
at that Place and marched about half a mild up to the Encampment, : It
bing Dark before we got in we ware obliged to Lay on the cold ground I
being unwell made it an uncomfortable Nights Rest to me.
