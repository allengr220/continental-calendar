Tuesday October 22"! I was on forteauge in gitting Timber into the
Lake—This Evening we have the news in the camp that the Indians
Killed five of our men with there Tomeyhawks Just below the mills
This Morning Died Josiah Parker at Casteltown on his way home
at was

Wednesday October y* 234 This Day I Rec* a Letter from Home
Dated y* 13" of october

fireing
being Thursday October y* 24" 1776 In the afternoon I was on forteauge
armey with a Party from our Reg'—This Morning Died in camp John Honey
of Wilton Belonging to Capt Manns comp” in Col? Reeds Reg'—
