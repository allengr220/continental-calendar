Bynarjar Beebe a soldier in the 5th Company Confined 
of propain Swaring was Brought before Sd Court &amp; on 
Examination plead Guilty of the Crime Laid to his 
Charge it is the opinon of Sd Court that Sd Beebe pay 
the Sum of one Shilling as a fine all Which fines are to 
be Dispofed of Agraeble to the Fifty firft Article of the 
Martial Law
