12s9 L:M

after we came into the camp I Paid for five half Points of Brandy
which was Drinkt by the whole of the Mess—7s L: M one Quart and
half a Pinte of westindia Rum
— —7 L: M one Quart and half a Pinte of Brandy at another time
which was Drank by this Mess at 4 four Shillings Pt Quart 5s L:M

I Lent Capt Barron rs Lawful money to Pay Jon* Greele.
