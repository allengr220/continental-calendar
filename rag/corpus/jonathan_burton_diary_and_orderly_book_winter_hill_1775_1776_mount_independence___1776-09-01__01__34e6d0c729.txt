Sunday Sept 1" 1776 this Day I was Returned unfit for Duty and
Drawed allowance with the Sick Men

Monday Sept" 2" this Day another Regt came in hear from Skeensborough, I wrote a Letter home

Tuesday Sept" 3" this Day I walked out about half a mild into the
woods but was Scarcely able to get in again I being so weak and feble—

Wednesday Sept" 4 this being my well Day I Walked out almost all
day, the Return of the Sick in our company 26 unfit for Duty—
