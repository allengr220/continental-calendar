Wednesday November 27" 1776 I Set away from N° 4 about ten
oclock in the morning and travled to walpole to Gooldsmiths Tavern
and there Lodg* that Night

Thursday November y° 28" I Sat of from Gooldsmiths in Walpole and
Travled to Marlborough and there Met with my Horse about Two
o clock in the afternoon at Tuckers Tavern from there we came to Dublin that Night and Log? at one Greenwoods a Privat House

Fryday Nov" 29" 1776 I Set of from Dublin Early in the Morning and
got to my owne house about three in the afternoon—
