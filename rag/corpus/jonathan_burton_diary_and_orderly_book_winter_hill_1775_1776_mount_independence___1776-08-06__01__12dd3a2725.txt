Tuesday August 6 we got to wood Creek to Major Skeens and crosed
the Creek about twelve oclock hear we Picht our tents or otherways
Built Booths with Brush and Lay on the ground Very well contented
with our New habitation though it hapned to be a very havey Shower
and in the Morning the Most of the Men wear as wet as so many
Drownded Rats :
