Tuesday Sept’ ro Camp News to day that all our fleet Down the
Lake was taken or Destroyed to a man—

This Day M' Joseph Stiles came in hear by him I Rec* a Letter
Dated y* 3" of Sept

Wednesday Sept’ 11" Camp News to Day thata party of our men
Wenton Shore from the fleet after foshiens [fascines] and were fired upon
by the Enemy three Killed and Six wounded—

this Day I wrote a Letter home and Sent it Cap' M*Keen

32

Thursday Sept" 12" this Day Every officer and Solder In the Reg' fit
for Duty (but those for Guard) was ordered on Forteage—

Fryday y* 13 of Sept’ this day Reubin Cumings Drumer of our company Departed this Life in the 16" year of his Age

Saturday Sept’ 14" nothing Meterial only we Rec the News in camp
of this fight at Newyork from the Boston News paper Dated in Septr.
(this Day Rec* of David Burton five Dollars)
