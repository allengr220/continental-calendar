Sunday October 27" 1776 we had the News in camp of a Victory won
caused by the Continental Troops over the Britans at New york.—In the aftert mid- noon there was an Alarm in camp ocasioned by Some boats being Dist outa covered Down the Lake This Day Ensign Gillmore of our company
home got his Discharge from the Army—
