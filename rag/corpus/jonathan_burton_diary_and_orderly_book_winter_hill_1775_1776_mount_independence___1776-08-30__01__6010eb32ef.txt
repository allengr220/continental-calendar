30

the Morning with a fair gale of Wind and about noon we came to the
mouth of ottercreek there we had to go nine mild up the creek there to
Load with hay but the hay not being Ready I could not Load my Boats
and was obliged to Lay there that night in my boats (This creek is about
forty Milds from Ticonderoga Down the Lake Toards St Johns)—
