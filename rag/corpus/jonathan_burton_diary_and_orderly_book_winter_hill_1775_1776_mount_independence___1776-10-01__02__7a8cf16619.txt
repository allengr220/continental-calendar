Saturday November y® 2™ was cold and Snowey and in the afternoon
a Scouting Party of 60 men went out from our Reg to See what Discoveries they could Make: this Day the Comm" came in from New Hampshire to appoint the officers for the New Establishment—

Sunday November y* 3" 1776 Thomas Killicut Medad Combs John
Fletcher &amp; Jon*Greele got a Discharge from the Armey and Set off for
home: this Day was Stole from my Dore one of my Cotten Shurts by a
bold Theefe

Monday Nov" y* 4" we have the news in camp of the Enemyes Leaying Crownpoynte—This Day I was on forteauge

Tuesday November y* 5" I took a Walk from our camp over the Lake
to ‘Ticonderoga and from there up into the out Lines.
