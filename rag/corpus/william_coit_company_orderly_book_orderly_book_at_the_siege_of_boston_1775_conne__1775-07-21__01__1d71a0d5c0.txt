Regm41 Court Martial held July 21th at Cap4 Silles 
Marque Cap4 Sill Lev4 Waterhoufe Lev4 Brulter Enfign 
hall Enfign Latimer Enfign Latham Members Tryd Dan11 
McCain a soldier in Cap4 Gailes Company Confined for 
Intemperate Drinking Spiritus Licquers when on guard 
for Swearing &amp; quariling and is found guilty of the 
Crimes alledged to his Charge Sad Court are of Opinon 
that the prifoner pay a fine of ten Shillings Lawfull

65

Money to be Disposed of Agreeable to the 51th article 
of the Martial Law &amp; be Returned to his Duty
