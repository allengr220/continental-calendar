Rigermental orders—John Rokes of Capt Spauldings company and
Col? Reeds Rigt Tryed ata late Court Martial for Giting Drunk when on
Gard—W hereof Capt Oliver was Presidant—The Prisnor confesing himself Guilty of breach of the 22" article of the Rules and Regulations
of the Contential—Therefore adjudged the S' John Roakes to Recive
Twenty lashes on his Naked Back but on account of his appearing very
humble and Penitant, Recomended that Sum Part of the Funishment be
Mitigated Therefore ordered that the S! John Roakes Receive Ten
Stripes only The Col? Approves the Sentence of the Court and orders
that the S* John Roakes Receive Ten Lashes on his naked back at Eleven
o clock this forenoon, the Adj' of the Rigt to See it Executed—

Israel Gilman Lt Cole
