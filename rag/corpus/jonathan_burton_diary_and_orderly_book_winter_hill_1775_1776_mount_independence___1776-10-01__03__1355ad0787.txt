Wednesday Nov" y* 6" 1776 I was on forteauge with a Party of our
Reg' In carring Piquets out of the woods up to the Piquet fort—This
Day one Regt of the New Jersey Troops were Dischargd and Set of from
this Place to Skeensborough and it was Reported in camp that they were
Drum! out of the camp by the Genr" order—

Thursday November y* 7"

Fryday November y* 8" I was on forteauge with a party of Men in

Saturday November y* 9" I drawd my Wages for the Month of October—

Sunday November y* ro The Mellitia Reg" Returned home that came
in on Acct of the Alarm :

Monday November y* 11" 1776 I Took a Walk Down to the Scotchmans to carry a Shurt to be washed—

Tuesday November ye 12" The Inveleads ware Draughted from the
