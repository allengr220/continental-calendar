5

and it is not Douted but the Captains and Subalterns will Immedetly
follow their Example—

The General by no means Recomends or Desires Officers to Run into
Costly or Expencive Regimentals, no matter how plain or coarse so that
they are but uniform in their color cut and fashion, ‘The officers belonging to those Regiments whose uniforms is not yet fixt upon had better
Delay making their Regimentals till they are—
