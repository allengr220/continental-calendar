6th. —- This morning three ships came in with reinforcements of
about one thousand men. Ali the bells in the town rang for joy most
of the day ; then all the forces marched over to Abram’s plains to have
a battle with our people, but they retreated as fast as possible, and left
av umber sick in the hospital, likewise some of their cannon and ammunition, \ th a number of small arms and packs.
