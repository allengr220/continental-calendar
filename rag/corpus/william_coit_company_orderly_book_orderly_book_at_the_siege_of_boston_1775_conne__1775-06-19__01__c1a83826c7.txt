General orders 19th June 1775

Parole Scarbouagh Countersign Brunfwick 
Field officer of the Day to Morrow Coln Woodbrigde 
Field Officer of the Picquit Night Majr Stacy 
Field officer of the Main guard to Morrow Lev4 Colo11 
Bruckit

Adjutant of the Day to Morrow Montagu

Regmential orders

That the Cap* of Each Company Make a Return of 
there Company therin mentioning the Number Fit for 
Duty Sick and wounded Mising and the Number of 
armes Lost and that there Be a Regmential Courtmarfhel 
Immeadiatly Sit in orders to try John Bray of Capt. 
Clarks Company Members for Sd Courtmarfhell one 
Captain and 4 Subaltains

*p orders General putnam 
Daniel Tyler Adjutant
