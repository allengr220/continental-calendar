__ Fryday Sept" 27 this Day came in to camp L‘ Butterfield by him I
Rec* a Letter from home and also Some Stores from my Wife

Saturday Sept’ 28 I wrote a letter and Sent it home by Lt Butterfield
who Sat of from this Place this morning for Wilton

Sunday Sept" 29 this Day Proved to Be a very Raney Day all Day—
we have no camp News to Day—
